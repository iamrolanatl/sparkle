## Conflict risk
- **Bare "SPARKLE" for a K-12 student/parent app with an AI assistant: HIGH.** Crowded field in classes 9/41/42, an identical pending mark in all three classes, a Viacom-owned SPARKLER for children's educational/progress software, and an AI-assistant SaaS "SPARKLE ASSISTANT". Registration of bare SPARKLE is likely to draw a 2(d) likelihood-of-confusion refusal or opposition, and "sparkle" is weak/diluted (hard to enforce against others).
- **Distinctive compound: LOW-MEDIUM** (no live hits for the candidates above), subject to counsel's full search.

## Options
1. **Compound brand (recommended):** e.g., **"Sparkle Student"**, **"SparkleEd"**, "Sparkle Scholar", "SparklePath" — keep "Sparkle" as the robot character's name inside the product. Counsel runs full clearance; file intent-to-use (1(b)) in classes 9, 42 (and 41 if offering educational services).
2. **Coined name** with Sparkle as mascot (strongest protection, more marketing work).
3. **Keep bare "Sparkle"** unregistered — highest risk of a later forced rename (domain, app stores, marketing spend).
Before buying the production domain (SA-07), pick the brand direction (SA-11).
